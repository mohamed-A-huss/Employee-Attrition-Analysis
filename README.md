# Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition patterns using Power BI to help HR professionals understand why employees leave and how various factors contribute to attrition. The dataset contains detailed information about 1,470 employees, including job role, job satisfaction, overtime, salary, promotion history, and more.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze the attrition rate across departments and job roles.
- Identify key drivers of attrition (e.g., job satisfaction, salary, overtime).
- Discover trends related to promotions, tenure, and distance from home.
- Provide actionable insights to improve employee retention.

---

## 📁 Dataset
- **Source:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance
- **Size:** 1,470 records
- **Format:** CSV
- **Features Include:**
  - Job Role
  - Department
  - Monthly Income
  - Years at Company
  - OverTime
  - Job Satisfaction
  - Attrition status
  - Distance From Home
  - Years Since Last Promotion

---

## 🧩 Key Visualizations
- **KPI Cards:** Total Employees, Total Attrition, Attrition Rate, Average Job Satisfaction
- **Pie Chart:** Attrition Rate by Department
- **Bar Charts:**
  - Attrition Rate by Job Role
  - Attrition by Job Satisfaction
  - Attrition by OverTime
- **Column Charts:**
  - Average Salary by Attrition
  - Attrition Number by Distance From Home
- **Line Charts:**
  - Attrition by Total Working Years
  - Attrition by Years At Company

---

## 📈 Insights
- Sales Representatives have the highest attrition rate at 40%.
- Employees with low job satisfaction and frequent overtime tend to leave more.
- Employees who haven't been promoted recently are more likely to leave.
- The average monthly income is lower for those who left the company.
